# Mandala Motion Language

OBSAD describes this family as **topology-preserving recursive radial metamorphosis**: a stable symmetry field whose motifs undergo phase-aligned substitution, scale recursion, chromatic migration, ringwise reconfiguration, and rhythm-responsive expansion while the visual center remains perceptually locked.

## Governed distinction

- **Observation** records only visible behavior and a bounded time/region claim.
- **Inference** proposes a mechanism and cites observation evidence.
- **Derived capability** turns supported behavior into a reusable operator.
- **Audio binding** is editorial or marker-based unless a measured analysis artifact is declared.

The four source binaries remain external. Their metadata and observation records live under `fixtures/motion/mandala-videos`; hashes remain pending governed ingestion.

## Operator families

`recursive_zoom`, `ring_replacement`, `sector_substitution`, `topology_preserving_morph`, `material_transmutation`, `chromatic_migration`, `counter_rotation_alignment`, `radial_wave`, `symmetry_order_transition`, `anchor_orbit`, and `aperture_revelation` are separately addressable operations. They should be combined only when topology locks and a clear final state survive the combination.

## Music-response strategies

- Atmospheric/dreamlike: slow recursive push, palette migration, center revelation.
- Wave/pulse/floating: delayed ring expansion and traveling phase.
- Ritual/meditative: symmetry convergence, crown emergence, harmonic breathing.
- Glitch/bass/808: quantized sector alternation, controlled fracture, exact realignment.
- Organic/coastal: tidal scale breathing, refraction, current-like counter-rotation.
- Tension/release: complementary ring exchange and expansion/contraction pairs.

These are editorial mappings, not claims that a track title or “432 Hz” label creates a unique physical visual response.

## Splice discipline

Match topology family, center, symmetry order, phase, scale velocity, palette, center motif, persistent anchors, and motion vector. Use at least 12 handle frames for generated transitions. A reversal must coincide with a declared musical or narrative event; otherwise preserve the outgoing motion vector.

## Platform workflow

Analyze or place markers first, generate topology-preserving segments second, validate entry/exit states third, and assemble exact cuts and platform-native audio in deterministic post-production.
